Localization QA for your Google Sheets

LocaLinter is SuperGaming's localization validator. Point it at the Google Sheet holding your game's translations and it flags formatting errors, missing locales, and variable mismatches across every language at once — then writes the fixes you approve straight back to the sheet.

  • Format issues Catches broken placeholders, stray tags, and spacing and punctuation problems in translated strings.
  • Missing locales Shows which keys are still untranslated, per language, so nothing ships half-localized.
  • Search across languages Finds any string in any locale and edits it in place, with changes synced back to the sheet.
  • Device scan Optionally drives a build on a real device to catch text that overflows or truncates on screen.

What LocaLinter asks for

Signing in with Google lets LocaLinter read your name, email, and profile picture to identify you, and read and write the specific Google Sheet you choose to open. It never browses the rest of your Drive, and it only writes to a sheet when you click save. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Access is limited to @supergaming.com work accounts.
