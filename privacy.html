LocaLinter.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

LocaLinter is an internal localization QA tool operated by SuperGaming for members of the supergaming.com Google Workspace domain. It checks localization spreadsheets for formatting errors, missing translations, and variable mismatches. This policy explains what data the tool touches, where that data goes, and how long it is kept.

The short version. LocaLinter runs in your browser. Your spreadsheet content travels directly between your browser and Google's APIs — it is never stored on a LocaLinter server. Your Google profile and access token live in your browser tab and are discarded when you close it. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train any model.

Who can use LocaLinter

Sign-in is restricted to Google accounts on the supergaming.com domain. If you sign in with any other account, the access token is revoked immediately and no data is retained.

Google account data we access

When you sign in with Google, LocaLinter requests these OAuth scopes:

  • userinfo.email and userinfo.profile — your email address, name, and profile picture. These identify you in the app's account menu and enforce the domain restriction above.
  • spreadsheets — read and write access to Google Sheets, used to load the localization sheet you select and to save corrections you explicitly choose to write back.

LocaLinter only opens the specific spreadsheet you load by URL or preset. It does not browse, index, or enumerate your Drive, and it never writes to a sheet unless you click a save or sync control.

How Google user data is used, stored, and shared

Google account data is used solely to authenticate you, display who is signed in, and authorize requests to the Google Sheets API on your behalf. It is not used for any other purpose.

  • Where it is stored. Your OAuth access token and basic profile are kept in your browser's sessionStorage. They are cleared when you sign out or close the tab.
  • Server storage. None. LocaLinter has no user database and no server-side session store. Spreadsheet requests go directly from your browser to sheets.googleapis.com.
  • Sharing. Google user data is not transferred to any third party, other than to Google itself to service your requests. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising, and it is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

LocaLinter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data stored in your browser

To keep the tool usable between visits, LocaLinter saves the following locally on your device. This data never leaves your browser.

  • IndexedDB — the most recently loaded sheet, so results survive a page refresh.
  • localStorage — preferences such as the active tab, search settings, ignore list, text-wrap state, and Quick Translate language choices.

Clearing your browser's site data for this domain removes all of it.

Quick Translate

The Quick Translate panel sends the text you type to a LocaLinter serverless endpoint (/api/translate), which forwards it to Google's public translation endpoint and returns the result. Only the text you enter and the chosen language pair are sent. The endpoint does not attach your identity, and it does not log or store the text.

Device scan

Device scan is optional and only works if you run the LocaLinter agent yourself on your own machine. When you do, the agent captures screenshots and on-screen strings from the device under test and sends them to the Anthropic Claude API for analysis, using an API key you supply. That traffic goes from your machine to Anthropic directly and is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy. Do not point a scan at a screen showing personal or confidential data you would not want sent to that service. If you never run the agent, no scan data is produced or transmitted.

Analytics and cookies

LocaLinter sets no advertising or tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics. The only browser storage it uses is described above. The hosting provider may keep standard server request logs for operational and security purposes.

Data retention and deletion

  • Sign out revokes the OAuth access token with Google and clears your token and profile from the tab.
  • Closing the tab discards your session automatically.
  • Clearing site data in your browser removes cached sheet data and preferences.
  • You can revoke LocaLinter's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Because LocaLinter stores no personal data on a server, there is nothing further for us to delete on your behalf.

Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. Access tokens are short-lived, scoped to the permissions listed above, and held only in tab-scoped storage. Access to the tool is limited to your company Google Workspace domain.

Children

LocaLinter is an internal workplace tool and is not directed to, or intended for use by, anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of LocaLinter after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about data handled by LocaLinter can be sent to privacy@supergaming.com.
